Introduction
This Privacy Policy clarifies the rules for collection, usage, storage, protection, and disclosure of user information when you download, access, and play the Merge Stack Journey mobile game and its supporting services (collectively the “Services”). This policy applies to all users accessing the game through Android devices and the Google Play platform. By continuing to use our Services, you fully consent to the information processing practices described in this policy.

1. Core Definitions
Services: Refers to the Merge Stack Journey game body, all in-game coin merging interactive functions, version updates, technical maintenance, and supporting service content.
Personal Data: Refers to any information that can directly or indirectly identify a specific individual user or be associated with a unique user identity.
Third-Party Services: Refers to external SDKs, statistical tools, advertising modules, and platform services not independently developed or operated by our team.
This Privacy Policy is an integral part of the Terms of Service and shall be used and interpreted in conjunction with the service terms.

2. Legal Basis for Data Processing
We process limited anonymous user data in compliance with global data protection regulations and legal requirements, based on the following legitimate grounds:
Service Operation Guarantee: To maintain the normal operation of coin merge game functions, optimize basic gameplay, and resolve user experience problems.
Legitimate Operational Interests: To analyze game operation data, adjust merge gameplay balance, optimize product performance, and upgrade user interactive experience.
Security Protection: To detect abnormal operations, cheating behaviors, unauthorized access risks, and ensure the overall security of game services.
User Consent: For data processing behaviors regulated by relevant laws, operations will be carried out based on your voluntary authorization, and you have the right to withdraw consent at any time.

3. Information Collection Scope
Merge Stack Journey adheres to the principle of minimal data collection and does not collect any sensitive personal privacy information. The collected information is limited to the following categories:
Information Voluntarily Provided by Users. This game does not require user registration, login, or real-name authentication. We only collect feedback content voluntarily submitted by users when contacting customer support.
Automatically Collected Anonymous Information. Basic anonymous device technical information, including device model, system version, and device language settings.
Anonymous game behavior data, including game session duration, coin merging interaction records, and local game progress.
Anonymous crash logs and operation exception data for technical troubleshooting and compatibility optimization.
Information from Third-Party Channels. If you authorize third-party platform services related to the game, we may obtain limited anonymous data in accordance with third-party privacy policies and your personal account settings, and no sensitive personal information will be obtained.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure Rules
We will not sell, trade, or leak user personal data for commercial purposes. We may only share anonymous, non-identifiable data with the following qualified parties to support normal service operation:
Technical service providers responsible for game hosting, data statistics, compatibility testing, and technical maintenance.
Authorized third-party SDK providers integrated in the game for basic service support.
Legal regulatory authorities, when required by legal provisions and official lawful requests.
All third-party parties involved in data processing have independent privacy compliance obligations and shall bear corresponding data protection responsibilities.

5. Cross-Border Data Transmission
The anonymous data collected by the game may be stored and processed in overseas server environments. For all cross-border data transmission behaviors, we will adopt standardized security protection measures in accordance with global data protection laws to ensure user information is fully protected and avoid unauthorized leakage and abuse.

6. Data Storage and Retention Cycle
User local game progress and preference data are permanently stored on the user's personal device and will not be uploaded to our cloud server. The anonymous technical data and operation logs collected by the server will only be retained within the time required for service optimization and technical maintenance. After the data loses operational value, we will actively delete or anonymize all relevant data. Aggregated non-identifiable statistical data can be retained for long-term product iteration analysis.

7. Data Security Protection Measures
We adopt industry-standard technical and organizational security measures to protect user anonymous data, including access permission control, data encryption storage, and real-time operation monitoring. We will try our best to prevent unauthorized access, tampering, disclosure, and loss of data. Nevertheless, no network security system can achieve absolute security, and we cannot guarantee 100% security of data transmission and storage.

In accordance with applicable data protection laws, you enjoy the following privacy rights:
The right to access and inquire about your relevant game data information.
The right to request correction or deletion of your valid data records.
The right to restrict or object to partial data processing activities.
The right to withdraw the data authorization you have granted at any time.
We will respond to all valid user data rights requests within 30 working days.

This game may display non-personalized advertisements by default to support free service operation. You can actively limit or turn off personalized advertising push by adjusting your Android device system settings or using the opt-out functions provided by third-party advertising partners.
